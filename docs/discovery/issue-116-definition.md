## Product objective

The portfolio exists to generate qualified interviews and paid software work.

The redesign must make Sebastián:
1. immediately understandable;
2. visually memorable;
3. technically credible;
4. easy to contact.

Beauty, motion and conversion are co-equal requirements. None may cancel the others.

## Primary audience

### Recruiter / talent partner
Needs to answer quickly:
- What role does he fit?
- Does he have real work?
- Is there enough evidence to continue?
- Can I send this profile to a hiring manager?
- Where is the CV/contact path?

### Engineering / hiring manager
Needs:
- ownership;
- architectural judgment;
- backend/data/integration depth;
- evidence of execution;
- honest limits;
- quality/reliability mindset.

### Secondary audience
Potential freelance or product client evaluating whether Sebastián can understand a workflow and turn it into working software.

## Content decisions

### Header
Keep:
- name;
- Work;
- About;
- Contact;
- CV/Resume;
- language switch.

Remove from primary navigation:
- Experience;
- Method.

They no longer exist as standalone Home destinations.

### Hero
Keep:
- role;
- backend orientation;
- core proposition;
- CTA to Selected Work;
- CV/Resume CTA;
- optional low-emphasis GitHub link;
- location/work mode as supporting metadata.

Remove:
- HMS screenshot;
- lead-project roster;
- project status list;
- stack list;
- long methodology explanation.

### Selected Work
Purpose: convert promise into proof.

Each project must expose without relying on hover:
- index;
- title;
- category/problem signal;
- one concise technical/value line;
- case-study action.

Interaction may add:
- approved evidence preview;
- status;
- short role/stack metadata.

### Operating Mindset
Replaces most of Capabilities + Experience + Method.

Three ideas only:
1. Understand systems
2. Build end-to-end
3. Verify boundaries

Operational/SAP/integration background appears as context, not as a second resume.

### About
Must become genuinely human and shorter.

Keep:
- Uspallata / Mendoza;
- curiosity and learning;
- mountain/running/outdoor context.

Technical background may appear only once and briefly.

### Additional Work
Keep six secondary cases but reduce visual weight.

It is an archive/breadth layer, not six additional hero cards.

### Contact
Must explicitly support job conversion:
- email;
- CV/Resume;
- GitHub;
- location/work mode;
- honest English level if retained.
