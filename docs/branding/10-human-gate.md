Decision owner: Sebastián Ojeda

## Decision

**APPROVED — Direction 01 / Systems Ledger with Product Proof restraint**

The selected palette and design direction is the warm **Stone / Andes Copper** family.

### Approved palette foundation
- Canvas / Stone: `#F4F1EA`
- Paper / Surface: `#FFFDFC`
- Ink: `#111820`
- Graphite: `#59636D`
- Copper: `#A94F1C`
- Copper Dark: `#843A13`

### Approved public hierarchy
1. Sebastián Ojeda
2. Full-Stack Software Developer
3. Short proposition derived from `Del proceso al sistema.`
4. Selected project evidence
5. Capabilities / operational context
6. Project Method
7. Contact

### Constraints carried into BUILD
- `Systems Ledger` remains an internal design-direction name, not public brand copy.
- Project screenshots remain visually stronger than branding decoration.
- No literal mountain/mining visual identity.
- No copied plate/index vocabulary from reference portfolios.
- Copper is a signal color, not a dominant fill everywhere.
- Mono typography remains secondary.
- Evidence is expressed structurally; avoid badge overload.
- Full-Stack Software Developer remains explicit in the first viewport.
- ES/EN, accessibility, responsive behavior, reduced motion and Lighthouse/release QA remain mandatory.

## Gate result

DESIGN: **APPROVED**
