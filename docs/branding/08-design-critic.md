# Branding — Independent Critic — DESIGN

Issue: #63
Reviewed artifact: `docs/branding/07-design-directions.md`
Role: Independent Critic / Verifier

## Verdict

**HUMAN GATE**

Two directions satisfy the Definition contract strongly enough to build; one should not proceed without rework.

- **A — Systems Ledger:** PASS / recommended
- **B — Operational Atlas:** REWORK
- **C — Product Proof:** PASS / safe alternative

## Direction A critique

### Why it passes
- Preserves recruiter category and first-viewport clarity.
- Evolves the existing visual tokens instead of discarding working UI decisions.
- Creates identity through a repeatable grammar rather than a logo gimmick.
- Matches the actual evidence: workflows, integrations, QA, method and operational software.
- Can be implemented incrementally and validated without large runtime dependencies.

### Conditions for BUILD
1. `Systems Ledger` is an **internal design-direction name**, not public brand copy.
2. Do not reproduce Myles Vanevery’s plate/index vocabulary or page taxonomy.
3. Project numbers may remain simple ordering devices but cannot become the central motif.
4. “Evidence/verified/validated” must not become a badge on every surface.
5. Product screenshots retain stronger visual weight than system-trace decoration.
6. Mono typography stays secondary.

## Direction B critique

### Why it needs rework
The proposition is strong, but the route/field/regional metaphor adds avoidable ambiguity. The portfolio could be read as mountain, field-service or mining-specific even though the target audience is broader Full-Stack/Backend hiring.

The useful part of B is **human warmth**, not the atlas metaphor. Those qualities can be introduced into A through typography, copy and photography/evidence treatment without importing a geographic visual identity.

## Direction C critique

### Why it passes
- Extremely clear and recruiter-friendly.
- Strongest screenshot hierarchy.
- Low accessibility and performance risk.
- Easy to maintain.

### Why it is not the first recommendation
Its differentiation depends mostly on content quality. It solves polish better than brand memorability and may end up resembling a high-quality but conventional developer/product portfolio.

## Copy critique

`Del proceso al sistema.` is the strongest short Spanish candidate because it is compact and aligns with the actual project narratives. It must be paired with `Full-Stack Software Developer` visibly; alone, it is too abstract.

`From workflow to system.` is semantically close in English but should be reviewed during implementation for naturalness. A possible Design-level alternative is `Turning real workflows into working systems.`; this should not be treated as final until ES/EN copy QA.

## Final recommendation

Approve **A — Systems Ledger**, with C’s screenshot-first restraint and the six conditions above.

That produces a portfolio that is more authored and memorable without making visual branding more important than the work.
