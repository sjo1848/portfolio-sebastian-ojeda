# Branding — DESIGN — Palette exploration II

Status: DESIGN / exploration
Issue: #63
Input: Definition PASS + Design Human Gate unresolved

This increment expands color exploration without authorizing BUILD. It keeps Direction A — Systems Ledger as the structural reference and tests alternative color personalities against the same recruiter/evidence constraints.

## Baseline A — Stone + Copper

- Canvas: `#F4F1EA`
- Surface: `#FFFDFC`
- Ink: `#111820`
- Graphite: `#59636D`
- Copper: `#A94F1C`
- Copper Dark: `#843A13`

Character: warm technical, evidence-led, mature.
Risk: copper can become visually predictable if overused.

---

## D — Mineral + Oxide

- Chalk: `#F3F1EC`
- Surface: `#FCFBF8`
- Carbon: `#20252A`
- Steel: `#697177`
- Oxide: `#98513A`
- Oxide Dark: `#713A2B`

Character: more industrial/material than A, but quieter and less orange.
Why test it: keeps the operational/system feeling while making the brand feel less like a standard copper-accent portfolio.
Risk: can become too subdued if typography and spacing do not carry enough personality.

---

## E — Ivory + Petrol + Brass

- Ivory: `#F6F2E8`
- Surface: `#FFFDF8`
- Deep Petrol: `#12343B`
- Slate: `#5D6B6E`
- Brass: `#B7791F`
- Brass Dark: `#825610`

Character: precise, technical and distinctive; less common than blue SaaS and less literal than copper/industrial branding.
Why test it: strong contrast, credible for software/integrations/operations, and memorable without becoming cyber.
Risk: petrol + brass can drift toward fintech/consulting if card composition becomes too corporate.

---

## F — Parchment + Oxblood

- Parchment: `#F5F0E8`
- Surface: `#FFFDFC`
- Charcoal: `#1D2024`
- Ash: `#686D73`
- Oxblood: `#8E3B46`
- Oxblood Dark: `#692B34`

Character: editorial, serious, authored and less obviously “tech palette”.
Why test it: strongest move away from generic developer aesthetics while preserving a restrained professional feel.
Risk: may read as editorial/fashion/cultural rather than engineering if technical structure is too soft.

---

## G — Cream + Midnight + Clay

- Cream: `#F7F3EA`
- Surface: `#FFFEFB`
- Midnight: `#17213A`
- Blue Gray: `#667085`
- Clay: `#C05A3B`
- Clay Dark: `#8E3F2C`

Character: digital/product-led with warm human contrast.
Why test it: gives stronger visual energy and works well around product screenshots while avoiding pure blue-SaaS monotony.
Risk: midnight + clay is more conventional and may reduce the systems/operations distinctiveness.

---

## H — Limestone + Forest + Ember

- Limestone: `#F3F0E7`
- Surface: `#FFFDF7`
- Forest Ink: `#20312D`
- Moss Gray: `#69746E`
- Ember: `#B55232`
- Ember Dark: `#7E3827`

Character: grounded, calm, technical-human.
Why test it: introduces subtle field/real-world warmth without using literal regional imagery.
Risk: green can push the identity toward sustainability/outdoors if used beyond structural accents.

## Comparative intent

These palettes are not separate brand strategies. They test the emotional range inside the approved positioning:

- A: warm technical / current evolution
- D: material / industrial restraint
- E: technical precision / distinct contrast
- F: editorial authorship
- G: product/digital energy
- H: grounded systems / human field context

## Next decision

Palette selection should happen together with typography and project composition, not from swatches alone. The strongest candidates should be mocked in the same hero + project-card composition before final Human Gate approval.
