## Additional critique

### Strength — positioning hierarchy is disciplined
The contract avoids replacing a searchable job category with a brand slogan. That is important for a professional portfolio.

### Strength — evidence remains the center of credibility
The branding system is explicitly subordinate to product evidence and case-study proof.

### Strength — visual constraints are specific without prematurely designing
The document preserves the current warm-neutral / ink / copper foundation as a hypothesis while leaving exact type, layout and motif execution to DESIGN.

### Watch item D1 — proposition wording is not yet public copy
`Convierto procesos reales en software end-to-end verificable` is strategically coherent but somewhat technical. DESIGN may test more natural public wording as long as meaning and role hierarchy remain intact.

### Watch item D2 — avoid over-branding “evidence”
Evidence is a strong principle, but repeating labels such as evidence/verified/validated everywhere could make the interface feel procedural. DESIGN should express proof visually and structurally, not only through badges.

### Watch item D3 — typography must not create a new runtime dependency without justification
A custom font can improve identity, but performance, licensing and availability need consideration. A direction should also work with robust fallbacks.
